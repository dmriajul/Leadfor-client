## Target filter applied

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Rating band A | 4.0 – 4.5 stars, any review count ≥ 10 |
| Rating band B | 4.6 – 5.0 stars **and** fewer than 50 reviews |
| Size | Established local operator, **not** a 500+ review market leader (One Hour 10,864 / Royal Air 9,002 / Abacus 11,794 excluded) |
| Result | **20 / 20 rows pass both the rating and the ≥10 review floor** |

Houston benchmark used throughout: the city's HVAC category averages **4.7 stars across 464 listed contractors (~169 reviews each)** — so a 4.4-rated company with 26 reviews is competing against 4.9-rated firms holding 230–290 reviews. That gap is the pitch.
